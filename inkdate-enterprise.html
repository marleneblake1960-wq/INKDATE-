Most people think InkDate is an AI image generator. It isn't. InkDate is a multi-stage AI pipeline that recreates history exactly as people experienced it.

Starting with nothing more than a date, a country and a newspaper, InkDate researches real historical events, writes authentic editorial, recreates period newspaper design, and generates a reconstructed front page that looks as though it came directly from the archive.
