Frequently Asked Questions

What dates can I choose?
Any date from 1850 to the present day across all supported languages and countries.
How quickly do I receive my newspaper?
Digital downloads are generated in approximately 30–45 seconds. Physical prints will be dispatched within 2–3 working days once Print & Post launches.
Is the content historically accurate?
We research real historical headlines for every date. For dates where exact records are unavailable, our AI generates period-accurate, historically plausible content. All newspapers carry an Inkdate attribution and are for gifting and keepsake purposes.
Can I get a refund?
Digital products are non-refundable once an image has been generated. Please check your details carefully before confirming — our app shows a full confirmation screen before generation begins.
What languages and countries are available?
Six languages — English, Français, Español, Deutsch, Português and Japanese — across 30+ countries including the UK, USA, Jamaica, Nigeria, South Africa, France, Germany, Brazil, Japan, Australia, Ireland, Canada, India and many more.
How does Print & Post work?
Print & Post is coming very soon. Your newspaper will be printed on premium museum-quality paper and posted directly to any address worldwide. Ready to frame and display.
