Questions

Frequently asked.

What dates can I choose? +
Any date from 1850 to the present day. We cover key historical events across all supported languages and countries, with AI-generated content for dates outside our curated archive.
How quickly do I receive my newspaper? +
Digital PDFs are delivered instantly by email. Physical prints are dispatched within 2-3 working days and delivered by tracked post within 5-7 working days to UK addresses.
Is the content historically accurate? +
We use carefully researched historical content for key dates across all supported languages. For other dates, our AI generates period-accurate content. All newspapers carry an Inkdate attribution and are for illustrative and gifting purposes — not for use as historical evidence.
Can I get a refund? +
Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded. Physical products that arrive damaged or defective will be replaced at no cost. Annual subscriptions can be cancelled within 14 days for a full refund.
What languages and countries are available? +
We support six languages — English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português and Japanese — across 30+ countries including the UK, USA, Germany, France, Spain, Brazil, Japan, Australia, Ireland, South Africa and many more.
How does the Memorial Edition work? +
You provide the date of birth and the date of passing, along with your choice of country and newspaper. We generate two photorealistic newspaper front pages and present them as a memorial certificate. By ordering, you confirm you are authorised to use the details of the deceased.
