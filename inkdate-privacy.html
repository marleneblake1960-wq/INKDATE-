2. What Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide Directly

When you use the Service, we may collect the following personal data:

  • Email address — when you sign up for early access, create an account, or contact us
  • Name — when you provide it as part of a gift certificate order
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe; we do not store card details
  • Date of birth or other significant dates — when you enter them to generate a newspaper
  • Name of a deceased individual — when ordering a Memorial Edition product
  • Delivery address — when ordering a physical print product
  • Gift message content — when included as part of an order

2.2 Data Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring website
  • Device type and operating system

We collect this data through cookies and similar tracking technologies including Google Analytics. See Section 8 for more information.

2.3 Sensitive Data

The name of a deceased individual entered for a Memorial Edition order is treated with the utmost sensitivity. We use it solely to fulfil your order and delete it in accordance with our retention schedule set out in Section 6.

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

3.1 To Provide the Service (Contractual Necessity)

  • To process your order and generate your historical newspaper
  • To deliver digital PDF files to your email address
  • To coordinate physical print fulfilment with our print partner
  • To process payment through Stripe
  • To send order confirmation and delivery updates

3.2 With Your Consent

  • To send you marketing communications where you have opted in
  • To use non-essential cookies and analytics tools

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us or using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

3.3 Legitimate Interests

  • To improve our Service based on usage patterns
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse
  • To respond to customer enquiries
  • To maintain the security of our systems

3.4 Legal Obligation

  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations, including tax and accounting requirements

4. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the following third parties and only to the extent necessary:

  • Stripe, Inc. — payment processing. PCI DSS compliant. Privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy
  • Gelato or Printful — physical print fulfilment. We share your delivery name and address only for print orders
  • Email service provider (Mailchimp or ConvertKit) — for transactional and marketing emails
  • Google LLC — analytics via Google Analytics. See Section 8
  • Anthropic, PBC — AI content generation. Dates and newspaper selections may be processed; we do not share names or personal details beyond what is technically necessary

Legal Requirements and Business Transfers

We may disclose your data if required by law or court order. If Inkdate is acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the new owner with prior notice to you.

5. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers are located outside the UK and European Economic Area (EEA), including in the United States. Where we transfer your personal data outside the UK/EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

For more information about specific safeguards, please contact us at privacy@inkdateapp.com.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Order records (name, email, dates, delivery address): 7 years from the date of order, to comply with UK tax obligations
  • Memorial Edition order details including name of deceased: 7 years from the date of order
  • Email addresses for marketing: until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Generated newspaper content: not retained after delivery unless you have an active subscription
  • Analytics data: 26 months (Google Analytics default)
  • Account data: for the duration of your account, plus 90 days after closure

7. Your Rights

UK and EU Users (GDPR)

  • Right of access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to restriction — request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@inkdateapp.com. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

California Residents (CCPA)

  • Right to Know — what personal information we collect, use and disclose
  • Right to Delete — request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out — we do not sell personal information
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — we will not discriminate for exercising your rights

To exercise California rights, contact us at privacy@inkdateapp.com with “CCPA Request” in the subject line.

Brazilian Users (LGPD)

If you are located in Brazil, you have rights under the LGPD substantially equivalent to GDPR rights. Please contact privacy@inkdateapp.com to exercise them.

8. Cookies

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the website to function. These cannot be disabled
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. You can opt out at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Preference cookies — to remember your settings

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. On your first visit we will ask for your consent before placing any non-essential cookies. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission via HTTPS
  • Payment processing through PCI DSS-compliant Stripe — we never store card details
  • Access controls restricting who can access personal data
  • Regular review of our security practices

No method of internet transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our Service is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU and UK). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under these ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

12. Changes to This Policy
